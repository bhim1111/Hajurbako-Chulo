Our Story

A heritage of flavor handed down through generations.

Hajurbako Chulo

At Hajurbako Chulo, we believe that food is more than just sustenance; it's a celebration of culture, family, and tradition. Our name, which translates to "Grandfather's Hearth," reflects our commitment to preserving the authentic flavors of homemade Nepali cuisine.

Every dish we serve is prepared using traditional recipes and the finest local ingredients, bringing the warmth and hospitality of a Nepali home to each of our guests.
